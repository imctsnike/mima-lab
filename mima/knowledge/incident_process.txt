MAJOR INCIDENT MANAGEMENT PROCESS

Overview:
This document outlines the standard major incident management process used to investigate, coordinate, communicate, and resolve critical service disruptions.

Incident Lifecycle:

1. Identification
- Receive user reports or alerts.
- Validate symptoms and affected services.
- Determine business impact.
- Assess severity.

2. Investigation
- Gather evidence from monitoring tools.
- Search for related incidents.
- Review recent changes and deployments.
- Check known problems and workarounds.
- Identify impacted applications, services, regions, and users.

3. Assignment
- Identify service owner.
- Identify responsible support teams.
- Engage technical responders.
- Establish collaboration channel if required.

4. Communications
- Publish Initial Communication.
- Provide periodic updates.
- Communicate business impacts.
- Share estimated restoration time when available.

5. Resolution
- Implement workaround or fix.
- Validate service restoration.
- Confirm user impact has been mitigated.
- Continue monitoring.

6. Closure
- Publish Resolved Communication.
- Confirm business recovery.
- Capture timeline of events.
- Schedule Root Cause Analysis if required.

Communication Guidelines:
- Use facts only.
- Avoid assumptions.
- Clearly state impact.
- Clearly state next actions.
- Clearly identify responsible teams.
- Provide confidence level when possible.

Severity Indicators:

High:
- Widespread user impact
- Revenue impact
- Consumer-facing outage

Medium:
- Partial degradation
- Limited user impact

Low:
- Minor issue
- Workaround available

Major Incident Roles:
- Major Incident Manager (MIM)
- Service Owner
- Application Support Team
- Infrastructure Team
- Vendor Support Teams

Success Criteria:
- Service fully restored
- Stakeholders informed
- Monitoring confirms stability
- Follow-up actions documented
